Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

"Hermes" is a private desktop application used only by the owner of the Hermes Mind YouTube channel to upload and manage videos on their own channel.

What data we access

With your permission, the app uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos, set thumbnails, update video descriptions and read basic channel information (channel name and subscriber count).

How data is stored

Authorization tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to, sold to, or shared with any third party. The app does not collect data about viewers.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
